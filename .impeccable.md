# Design context — SaaS Made Free

## Target audience

Developers and technical operators who pay for SaaS subscriptions and wonder which of
them they could rebuild themselves with a coding agent. They are sceptical by default and
they check: they will read `extension/content.js` before believing a privacy claim, and
they will rebuild the extension and compare the SHA-256 before trusting a download. A
second, larger audience arrives from search in seven languages looking for one specific
tool — they are less technical and less patient.

## Use cases

- Decide whether a subscription can be dropped, and what is actually lost by dropping it.
- Get a prompt that works, and hand it to their own coding agent.
- Verify that the project is not quietly an affiliate site.

## Brand personality and tone

**Honest to the point of being unhelpful to itself.** Only 30 % of entries are a clean
"yes"; 58 % are "you can build it but you lose real things"; 12 % are "keep paying". The
product says so before it says anything else. This is the entire differentiator: the
name promises free, and the site immediately qualifies the promise.

The register is a document, not a pitch. Verdicts, sources, dates. No urgency, no
exclamation, no growth-marketing voice. If a sentence could appear on a landing page
selling a course, it is wrong for this project.

## Visual direction

Editorial and typographic. Warm paper, ink, generous measure, left-aligned. The only
saturated colour in the whole interface is the verdict itself — green, amber, red — and
it is never used decoratively.

Explicitly avoided, because they read as marketing or as generic AI output: dark
backgrounds with glowing accents, gradient text, card grids, centred hero layouts,
oversized rounded icons above headings, glassmorphism.

The browser extension is the exception and is deliberately the opposite: dark, compact,
sitting on top of someone else's page where it must not be mistaken for the host site.

## Constraints

Static, no build step for the public pages, no external requests of any kind (no CDN
fonts, no analytics). Served by Cloudflare Pages. Must work with JavaScript disabled.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`.
